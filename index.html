About

The producer.
The place.
The story.

I'm based in Poznań and originally from Norte de Santander, Colombia—a region producing exceptional specialty arabica at 1,200–1,900m altitude.

I work directly with smallholder farmers I know personally, bringing traceable, relationship-based coffee to Polish roasters. No brokers. No middlemen. Just quality and transparency.

Every lot I source comes with full farm-level traceability — so you know exactly where your green coffee grew, who harvested it, and how it was processed.
